Check a message

Upload a screenshot or paste the text. SheShield looks for threats, blackmail and harassment, then shows what to do about it.

Drop a screenshot here, or click to choose

PNG or JPG, up to 8 MB. You can also paste an image with Ctrl+V.

The image itself is never stored. Only the text found in it, the details detected, and the image's fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash) are kept.

This text was read automatically from your screenshot and may contain mistakes. Check it against the image and correct anything that looks wrong, then analyze again.

Results are likelihoods, not verdicts. You decide what happens next.

Evidence vault

A private archive on this computer. Every record is linked to the one before it, so a change to any saved item can be detected.

What is stored

  • The message text, severity and score
  • The time it was saved (UTC)
  • A fingerprint of the screenshot, if there was one
  • Phone numbers, links and handles found in it
  • The screenshot image itself is not stored

What verification proves

  • Saved records have not been edited, removed or reordered since saving
  • It does not prove who sent a message
  • It does not prove the screenshot is genuine
  • Blockchain anchoring adds an outside timestamp. See Blockchain proof

Saved evidence

Blockchain proof

Publish a fingerprint of your vault to a public blockchain. Anyone can later see that your evidence existed at that time. Your messages, names and images are never sent.

How this works

i

Fingerprint, not content

Only the latest vault hash, a 64-character code, is published. It cannot be turned back into your messages.

ii

Test network

This app uses Ethereum Sepolia, a practice network. Transactions use free test coins, never real money.

iii

Checked, not assumed

A record is only marked verified after your wallet reads the transaction from the network and its data matches your vault.

5. What to do next

  • Do not delete the messages. Keep the original screenshots and note dates and times.
  • Report online at the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: cybercrime.gov.in
  • National Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930
  • Women Helpline: 181
  • Emergency (police): 112
  • Give the police the phone numbers and handles shown above. They can request the owner's details from the telecom operator or platform.
  • Tell a trusted person. You do not have to handle this alone.

Block the sender only after you have saved the evidence.

Complaint report

Create a PDF you can hand to the police or upload to the cyber crime portal. It is built from what is in your vault right now.

Report contents

  • Summary with highest severity and vault integrity result
  • Timeline of every saved item
  • Full evidence text with hashes
  • Blockchain transaction reference, if you anchored
  • Reporting steps and helpline numbers

The report contains no names of accused people. SheShield never identifies who owns a number or account.
